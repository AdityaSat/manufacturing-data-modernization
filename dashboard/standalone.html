A random forest combines sensor readings with temperature gap, mechanical power, and wear × torque. The baseline uses three fixed single-sensor rules.

Precision: true failures among alerts. Recall: captured failures among all failures. Average precision summarizes the full score ranking.

How the experiment is separated

Contiguous UDI blocks limit adjacent-row mixing. UDI order is only a sequence proxy. A real pilot needs timestamped, asset-separated evaluation.

Threshold explorer

Scores are uncalibrated. A score of 0.7 does not mean a validated 70% real-world failure probability.

Data and model controls

  • Exclude identifiers, the target, and all five failure-mode labels from model inputs.
  • Fit preprocessing and the model on training data. Choose the threshold using validation F2 with a 10% alert-capacity ceiling.
  • Keep the test set separate from fitting and threshold selection. Report false positives and missed failures.
  • Validate lead time and intervention outcomes in shadow mode before connecting alerts to maintenance actions.

What the estimate includes

Implementation includes connectivity, integration, development, training, and contingency. Recurring costs include the platform, support, and an additional inspection allowance for predictive maintenance.

Benefits start after a three-month pilot, reach 50% in months 4–6, and reach 100% in month 7. Recurring costs start in month 1. The model discounts monthly cash flows at a 10% effective annual rate.

Use contribution margin or avoidable loss, not gross revenue. The loss estimate must avoid counting the same line stoppage against multiple machines. Taxes, financing, terminal value, and inflation are excluded. No separate labor or energy savings are added.

04 / ROLLOUT PLAN

A 90-day pilot
with a decision gate.

Recommend a centralized monitoring foundation and a limited predictive maintenance pilot. Release the remaining rollout budget only after factory evidence supports the incremental investment.

Proposed implementation roadmap

DAY 01–30

Establish the baseline

Operations and the data engineer map critical assets, reconcile downtime records, and land sensor readings in a governed table.

Gate: ≥95% required data completeness and an agreed downtime cost definition.

DAY 31–60

Run in shadow mode

The reliability engineer reviews silent alerts. Record false alarms, usable warning time, and whether an intervention was possible.

Gate: Proposed pilot targets: ≥70% recall, ≥50% precision, and ≥24 hours actionable lead time. These are unvalidated targets.

DAY 61–90

Decide whether to scale

Finance compares attributable avoided downtime with actual platform, inspection, and implementation costs. The plant manager owns the decision.

Gate: Positive incremental NPV under conservative assumptions and an acceptable maintenance workload.
